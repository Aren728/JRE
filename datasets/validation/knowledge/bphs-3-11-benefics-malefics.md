# BPHS ch. 3 v. 11 — Benefices and malefics

- **Source**: Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra (BPHS)
- **Edition**: tr. R. Santhanam, Ranjan Publications, 2001 — Chapter 3
  ("Planetary Characters and Description"), v. 11
- **Verifies**: `facts.json` → `nature`

## Excerpt (verbatim)

> 11. BENEFICES AND MALEFIC: Among these, the Sun, Saturn, Mars, decreasing
> Moon, Rahu and Ketu (the ascending and the descending nodes of the Moon) are
> malefic while the rest are benefices. Mercury, however, is a malefic if he
> joins a malefic.

## Reading used by the facts

- MALEFIC: SUN, SATURN, MARS, RAHU, KETU (the waning/waning-Moon nuance is a
  degree-level refinement not modelled at sign granularity).
- BENEFIC: MOON, JUPITER, VENUS.
- NEUTRAL: MERCURY (benefic unless it joins a malefic — encoded as the
  conditional's neutral state).
