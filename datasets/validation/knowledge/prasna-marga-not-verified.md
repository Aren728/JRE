# Praśna Mārgam — negative evidence for the two NEEDS-RESEARCH rules

- **Source**: Praśna Mārgam
- **Edition**: tr. B. V. Raman (checked from the archive.org scan of the Raman
  translation), 1990
- **Applies to**: `bphs.budhaditya.2` (also see below) and
  `prasna-marga.moon-lagna.6` in `rules:yoga.json` — both held **INACTIVE**

## What the cited verses actually say

The original authored rules cited Praśna Mārgam ch. 1 v. 9 (a movable-sign
Moon as the prasna indicator) and ch. 3 v. 11 (Moon-Venus in the 7th from the
Chandra lagna). The actual verses do **not** support those claims:

- **Ch. 1 v. 9** concerns the *divisions of astrology* (Pramana/Phala
  departments), not a movable-sign Moon indicator.
- **Ch. 3 v. 11** is part of the *Lagna Sphuta / Kunda* calculation, not a
  Moon-Venus seventh-house rule.

No supporting passage for either rule was found in the text during VALIDATOR.

## Status

Per the recovery mandate, neither rule was silently rewritten to match some
other verse. Both remain **INACTIVE (NEEDS-RESEARCH)** with the unverified
citation preserved in `provenance.commentary` until a Rules-agent authoring
pass commits genuine reference excerpts. The same policy applies to
`bphs.budhaditya.2` (no Sun-Mercury "Budha-Aditya" yoga by that name was found
in the Santhanam BPHS text — ch. 25 is "Effects of Non-Luminous Planets").
