# Phaladīpikā ch. 2 (note following v. 23) — aspect strength

- **Source**: Phaladīpikā (Mantreśvara)
- **Edition**: tr. Dr. G. S. Kapoor (Ranjan Publications; undated scan) — Chapter 2,
  note following v. 23, p. 22
- **Verifies**: `facts.json` → `aspect_strength_positions` and
  `special_aspects` (cross-source agreement with BPHS ch. 26 v. 2-5)

## Excerpt (verbatim)

> All planets cast a quarter glance at the 3rd and 10th houses, half a glance
> at the 5th and 9th houses, three-quarters of a glance at the 4th and 8th
> houses and full glance at the 7th house. However, the aspects of Saturn on
> the 3rd and 10th houses from him, of Jupiter on the 5th and 9th houses from
> him and of Mars on the 4th and 8th houses are also treated as full aspects.

## Reading used by the facts

- QUARTER = {3, 10}; HALF = {5, 9}; THREE_QUARTER = {4, 8}; FULL = {7}.
- Special full aspects: Saturn {3, 10}, Jupiter {5, 9}, Mars {4, 8}.

Identical to the BPHS ch. 26 v. 2-5 doctrine — a cross-source confirmation.
