## Section A: Transit Planet to Nakshatra Activation

Research on classical texts regarding a transit planet occupying a Nakshatra and its relationship to the Nakshatra's ruling planet.

### Key Classical Framework

**Primary Transit Reference Frame**: All classical texts agree that transit results are primarily judged from the **Moon's natal position** (Janma Rashi/Janma Nakshatra). This is the single most important reference point for transit analysis.

- **Phaladeepika Ch.26, V.1**: "Of all the Lagnas it is only the Moon's Lagna that is most important for ascertaining the Gocharaphala (effects of transits)."
- **BPHS Ch.20**: Transits must be analyzed from the position of the Moon (Janma Rashi) to understand impact on mental and emotional well-being.
- **Saravali Ch.36**: "Effects of Planetary Transits" — transit results counted from natal Moon.

### Classical Framework for Nakshatra Lord Analysis

The natal condition of a planet is analyzed through:
1. **Dignity** (Ucha/Neecha/Swa) — exaltation, debilitation, own sign
2. **House placement** — which Bhava the planet occupies
3. **Conjunctions** — which planets are joined
4. **Aspects** — which planets aspect the Nakshatra lord
5. **Avastha** — planetary states (BPHS Ch.36)
