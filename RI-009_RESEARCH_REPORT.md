## Section A: Core Panchanga Traits

### Key Classical Framework

**Primary Sources**: BPHS Ch.2-3, Phaladeepika Ch.2-3, Saravali Ch.1-4, Brihat Jataka Ch.2-3

**Important Distinction**: Classical texts describe **physical characteristics** and **general nature** from Lagna/Rashi, NOT modern personality types. Terms like "wrathful," "steady," "generous," "fickle" are classical. Terms like "introvert," "extrovert," "analytical thinker" are modern.
