## Section 4: Error Attribution Hypotheses

The following hypotheses are derived from the raw pipeline output without proposing fixes.

### Hypothesis 1: Chain Impact Is Systematically Negative
- **Observation:** Average chain impact = -24.218667 (264/390 negative)
- **Possible cause:** The multi-hop chain evaluator (Layer 1.5) may be weighting dispositorship chains through dusthana houses too heavily. When a yoga-forming planet's dispositor chain passes through 6th/8th/12th houses, the negative functional role weight compounds across hops.
- **Impact:** This suppresses dynamic_strength for otherwise well-formed yogas, reducing the apparent prediction accuracy.

### Hypothesis 2: Vipareeta Raja Over-Triggering
- **Observation:** Vipareeta Raja yoga triggered in 7/50 charts (14%)
- **Possible cause:** The Vipareeta Raja detection logic may be too broad. Any dusthana lord (6th, 8th, 12th) placed in a dusthana triggers it, which is statistically common given that 12 houses contain all 9 planets.
- **Impact:** Non-specific signal — fires for nearly every chart regardless of whether the subject experienced the characteristic 'reversal through adversity' pattern.

### Hypothesis 4: Dasha Activation Misses on Key Events
- **Observation:** 65/150 events had no relevant yoga activated by the active Dasha lords
- **Possible cause:** The expected_planets in the fixture define which planets *should* be active, but the Dasha multiplier only fires when the MD/AD/PD lord *is* one of the yoga's involved planets. If the expected planet is not involved in any formed yoga, the activation check cannot succeed by construction.
- **Impact:** This is a structural limitation of the current evaluation framework — it tests yoga-level activation, not planet-level Dasha presence.

## Methodology

This report is generated **without any post-hoc calibration**. The pipeline was executed with frozen weights and default configuration.

### Pipeline Layers:
1. **Layer 1 — Relationship Graph:** Structural detection (conjunctions, aspects, dispositorship, exchanges, nakshatra edges)
2. **Layer 1.5 — Chain Evaluator:** Multi-hop Kendra-Trikona chain impact
3. **Layer 2 — Modifiers:** 5-tier priority (combustion, debilitation, graha yuddha, retrograde, node taint)
4. **Layer 3 — Temporal:** Vimshottari Dasha multiplier (transit inactive without ashtakavarga data)
5. **Layer 4 — Varga:** D9 (Navamsha) confirmation

### Dasha Activation Multipliers (frozen):
- MD lord matches yoga planet → **1.50×**
- AD lord matches yoga planet → **1.25×**
- PD lord matches yoga planet → **1.10×**
- No match (dormant) → **0.40×**
